---
title: Instant Payouts
category: Payouts
---

Instant Payouts send funds to an eligible debit card in about 30 minutes, any day of the week.

**Eligibility**
* Your account must be at least 30 days old.
* You need an eligible US debit card linked under Settings, then Payout methods.

**Fees and limits**
* Fee: 1.5% of the payout amount, with a minimum fee of $0.50.
* Limit: up to $10,000 per payout and $50,000 per day.

**How to send one**
Go to Balances, click Pay out now, choose Instant, and confirm. If the Instant option is grey, your account or card is not yet eligible.
