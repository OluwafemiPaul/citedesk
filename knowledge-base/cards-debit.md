title: Ledgerly business debit card
category: Cards
---

The Ledgerly business debit card lets you spend your Ledgerly balance anywhere Visa is accepted.

**Getting a card**
Go to Cards, then Order card. Physical cards arrive in 7 to 10 business days. A virtual card is available immediately.

**Fees and limits**
* No monthly fee and no fee for US purchases.
* Foreign transactions: 1% fee.
* ATM withdrawals: $2.50 per withdrawal at out of network ATMs, plus any fee from the ATM owner.
* Default daily spending limit: $10,000. Daily ATM limit: $1,000.

**Controls**
Admins can set spending limits for each card, freeze a card instantly, and get alerts for every purchase.
