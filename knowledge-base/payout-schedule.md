---
title: When will I get my payouts?
category: Payouts
---

Ledgerly sends your available balance to your linked bank account on a rolling schedule.

**Standard schedule**
* New accounts: payouts arrive 7 business days after a payment is captured, for your first 30 days.
* After 30 days with no unusual activity: payouts arrive 2 business days after capture.
* Payments captured after 5:00 PM Central are treated as captured the next business day.

**Weekends and holidays**
Banks do not process transfers on weekends or US federal holidays, so payouts scheduled on those days arrive the next business day.

**Where to see your schedule**
Go to Dashboard, then Balances, then Payouts. Each payout shows its expected arrival date and the payments it includes.

If a payout is later than its expected arrival date, see "Why is my payout delayed or on hold?".
