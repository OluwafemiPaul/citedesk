---
title: Changing your payout bank account
category: Account
---

Only account owners and admins can change the payout bank account.

**Steps**
1. Go to Settings, then Payout methods.
2. Click Add bank account and enter the routing and account numbers, or connect your bank instantly.
3. Confirm with your two factor code.
4. Set the new account as default.

**Security hold**
For your protection, payouts to a newly added bank account are paused for 3 business days, and every account owner receives an email about the change. If you did not make this change, contact support immediately and see "Suspicious activity, phishing and fraud".

The bank account must be a US checking account in the name of your business or the business owner.
