---
title: Suspicious activity, phishing and fraud
category: Security
---

**If you think your account was accessed by someone else**
1. Reset your password immediately.
2. Go to Settings, then Security, and click Sign out of all devices.
3. Freeze your cards under Cards.
4. Contact a human support agent right away so we can review recent activity and pause payouts if needed.

**Phishing**
Ledgerly emails always come from @ledgerly.example. We will never ask for your password, 2FA code, full card number or PIN. Forward suspicious emails to phishing@ledgerly.example and delete them.

**Fraudulent payments from customers**
If you think a customer used a stolen card, refund the payment before it becomes a chargeback, and do not ship the order.
