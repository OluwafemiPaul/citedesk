---
title: Two factor authentication and login security
category: Security
---

Two factor authentication (2FA) is required for every Ledgerly account.

**Supported methods**
* Authenticator app (recommended)
* Security key
* Text message (SMS)

**Change your method**
Go to Settings, then Security, then Two factor authentication.

**Lost your phone?**
Use one of the backup codes you saved when you set up 2FA. If you do not have backup codes, a human support agent can help you recover your account after verifying your identity. Recovery takes up to 2 business days.

**Password reset**
Click Forgot password on the login page. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

Ledgerly will never ask for your password or 2FA code by phone, email or chat.
