---
title: Chargebacks and disputes
category: Payments
---

A chargeback (also called a dispute) happens when your customer asks their card issuer to reverse a payment.

**What happens**
* The disputed amount plus a $15 dispute fee is held from your balance.
* You have 7 days to submit evidence, such as receipts, shipping tracking, or messages with the customer.
* The card issuer usually decides within 60 to 75 days.

**How to respond**
Go to Payments, then Disputes, open the dispute, and click Submit evidence.

**Outcomes**
* If you win, the amount and the $15 fee are returned to you.
* If you lose, the amount and fee are not returned.

**Tips**
Use a clear business name on statements, respond to customers quickly, and keep proof of delivery. Accounts with a dispute rate above 0.75% may be reviewed.

Ledgerly support cannot decide disputes. Only the card issuer can.
