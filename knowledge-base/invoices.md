---
title: Sending invoices
category: Payments
---

Create and send invoices that customers can pay by card or bank transfer.

**Create an invoice**
1. Go to Invoices, then New invoice.
2. Add the customer, line items, due date and any notes.
3. Click Send. Your customer receives an email with a secure payment link.

**Reminders and late fees**
Turn on automatic reminders to email the customer 3 days before and 1 day after the due date. You can also add a flat late fee.

**Fees**
Invoice payments use the standard pricing for card or ACH payments. There is no extra fee for sending invoices.

**Marking an invoice paid**
If a customer paid outside Ledgerly (for example, by check), open the invoice and click Mark as paid.
