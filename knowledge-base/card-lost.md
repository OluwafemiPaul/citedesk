---
title: Lost or stolen card
category: Cards
---

If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it right away.

**Freeze your card**
1. Open the Ledgerly app or Dashboard.
2. Go to Cards and select the card.
3. Click Freeze card. Frozen cards cannot be used until you unfreeze them.

**Replace your card**
If you cannot find the card, click Replace card. The old card is cancelled permanently and a new card with a new number arrives in 7 to 10 business days. A new virtual card is available right away.

**Unrecognized charges**
If you see purchases you did not make, contact a human support agent. They can open a card dispute for you. Report unauthorized charges within 60 days of the statement date.
