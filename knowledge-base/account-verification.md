---
title: Verifying your business (KYC)
category: Account
---

Financial regulations require Ledgerly to verify every business and its owners before you can receive payouts. This process is often called KYC (Know Your Customer).

**What we ask for**
* Legal business name, address and EIN (or SSN for sole proprietors).
* Name, date of birth and home address of each owner with 25% or more ownership.
* A government issued photo ID for the main account representative.

**How long it takes**
Most businesses are verified within 1 business day. Some need a manual review, which takes up to 5 business days.

**If verification fails**
You will get an email explaining what is missing. Upload the requested document under Settings, then Verification. See "Accepted verification documents" for what we accept.
