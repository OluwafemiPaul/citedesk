---
title: Why is my payout delayed or on hold?
category: Payouts
---

Most delays have one of these causes:

1. **Bank processing time.** Your bank can take up to 1 extra business day to post the deposit after Ledgerly sends it.
2. **Verification needed.** If we need more information about your business, payouts pause until verification is complete. You will see a banner in your Dashboard.
3. **Risk review.** A sudden spike in sales, a high refund rate or many chargebacks can trigger a review. Reviews usually finish within 3 business days.
4. **Bank account problem.** If your bank rejected the payout (for example, the account was closed), the funds return to your Ledgerly balance within 5 business days.
5. **Negative balance.** Refunds or chargebacks can reduce your balance below zero, which pauses payouts until the balance is positive.

Support agents can check the status of a specific payout for you. Contact a human agent with the payout ID (it starts with "po_").
