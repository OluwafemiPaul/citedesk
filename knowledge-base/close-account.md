---
title: Closing your Ledgerly account
category: Account
---

Only the account owner can close a Ledgerly account.

**Before you close**
* Wait for all pending payments and refunds to finish.
* Resolve any open disputes. You cannot close an account with open disputes.
* Pay out your remaining balance.
* Download any statements and tax documents you need.

**How to close**
Go to Settings, then Account, then Close account, and follow the steps.

**After closing**
* Cards stop working immediately.
* We keep records for 7 years as required by law.
* You can reopen the account within 30 days by contacting support. After that, you will need to sign up again.
