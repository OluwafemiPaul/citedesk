---
title: Accepted verification documents
category: Account
---

**Identity (for people)**
* US driver license or state ID
* US or foreign passport
* US permanent resident card

Photos must be in color, show all four corners, and not be expired.

**Business (for companies)**
* IRS EIN confirmation letter (CP 575 or 147C)
* Articles of incorporation or organization
* A business license issued by your state or city

**Address**
* A utility bill or bank statement from the last 90 days

**File rules**
JPG, PNG or PDF, under 10 MB each. Do not upload screenshots of documents or documents with parts covered.

Never send documents by email or chat. Always upload them under Settings, then Verification.
