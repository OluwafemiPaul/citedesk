---
title: Sending ACH and wire transfers
category: Transfers
---

You can send money from your Ledgerly balance to any US bank account.

**ACH transfers**
* Free.
* Arrive in 1 to 3 business days.
* Daily limit: $100,000.

**Domestic wires**
* $15 per wire.
* Arrive the same business day if sent before 2:00 PM Central.
* Daily limit: $250,000.

**International wires** are not supported yet.

**How to send**
Go to Transfers, click Send money, add or choose a recipient, enter the amount and confirm with your two factor code. New recipients may require an extra review the first time.

Wires cannot be cancelled once sent. Always confirm recipient details by phone if you received them by email.
