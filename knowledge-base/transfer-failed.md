---
title: Failed or returned transfers
category: Transfers
---

A transfer can fail or be returned by the receiving bank.

**Common reasons**
* Incorrect account or routing number
* Recipient account is closed or frozen
* Name on the account does not match
* The receiving bank rejected the transfer

**What happens next**
Returned ACH transfers come back to your Ledgerly balance within 5 business days. Returned wires can take up to 10 business days, and the $15 wire fee is not refunded.

**What you can do**
Check the recipient details under Transfers, then Recipients, and send a new transfer. If the funds have not returned after these timelines, contact a human support agent with the transfer ID (it starts with "tr_").
