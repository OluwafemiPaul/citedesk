---
title: Why was my card declined?
category: Cards
---

Common reasons a Ledgerly card is declined:

* **Not enough balance.** The card spends your available Ledgerly balance.
* **Spending limit reached.** Check the card's daily limit under Cards.
* **Card frozen.** Unfreeze it under Cards.
* **Merchant category blocked.** Admins can block categories such as gambling.
* **Wrong details.** Check the expiry date, CVC and billing ZIP code.
* **Suspected fraud.** Our systems may block an unusual purchase. You will get a text or app alert asking you to confirm it.

If none of these apply, contact a human support agent with the date, amount and merchant name. For your security, support will never ask for your full card number or PIN.
