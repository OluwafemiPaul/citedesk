---
title: Statements, 1099K and tax documents
category: Billing
---

**Monthly statements**
Go to Reports, then Statements to download PDF or CSV statements for any month.

**Form 1099K**
If your business processed payments that meet IRS reporting thresholds, Ledgerly issues a Form 1099K by January 31 for the previous year. Download it from Reports, then Tax documents. A copy is also sent to the IRS.

**Correcting tax information**
Update your legal name or tax ID under Settings, then Verification. Changes after a 1099K is issued require a corrected form, which a human support agent can request for you.

Ledgerly cannot give tax advice. Please talk to a tax professional about how to report your income.
