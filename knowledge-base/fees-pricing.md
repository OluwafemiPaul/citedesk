---
title: Pricing and fees
category: Billing
---

Ledgerly has no monthly fee on the Starter plan.

**Card payments**
* Online card payments: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction.
* In person card payments: 2.7% + $0.05 per transaction.
* International cards: add 1.5%.
* Currency conversion: add 1%.

**Bank payments (ACH)**
* 0.8% per ACH payment, capped at $5.

**Other fees**
* Chargeback fee: $15 per dispute. Refunded if you win the dispute.
* Instant Payouts: 1.5% (minimum $0.50).
* Outgoing domestic wire: $15. Outgoing ACH transfer: free.

**Growth plan**
The Growth plan costs $49 per month and lowers online card pricing to 2.6% + $0.30.

Refunds are free, but the original processing fee is not returned.
