---
title: Adding team members and roles
category: Account
---

You can invite teammates and control what they can do.

**Roles**
* **Owner:** full access, including closing the account. Cannot be removed.
* **Admin:** everything except closing the account and changing owners.
* **Developer:** API keys and webhooks, no access to payouts or bank accounts.
* **Analyst:** view only access to payments and reports.
* **Card holder:** can use their own card only.

**Invite someone**
Go to Settings, then Team, click Invite, enter their email and pick a role. Invites expire after 7 days.

The Starter plan includes up to 3 team members. The Growth plan includes unlimited team members.
