---
title: Issuing refunds to customers
category: Payments
---

You can refund a payment in full or in part for up to 180 days after the original charge.

**How to refund**
1. Go to Payments and open the payment.
2. Click Refund, enter the amount, and choose a reason.
3. Click Confirm.

**What to expect**
* Refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your customer's statement.
* The refund amount is deducted from your Ledgerly balance. If your balance is too low, it is deducted from your linked bank account.
* Ledgerly does not charge a fee for refunds, but the original processing fee is not returned.
* A refund cannot be cancelled once it is submitted.

If a customer has already opened a chargeback, do not refund the payment. Respond to the dispute instead (see "Chargebacks and disputes").
