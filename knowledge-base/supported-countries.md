---
title: Supported countries and currencies
category: Account
---

**Where Ledgerly is available**
Ledgerly accounts are available to businesses registered in the United States with a US bank account.

**Accepting international payments**
You can accept cards issued in most countries. International cards cost an extra 1.5%, and paying in a currency other than US dollars adds a 1% conversion fee.

**Currencies**
* You can charge customers in 35 currencies.
* Your balance and payouts are always in US dollars.

**Not supported**
* Businesses registered outside the US
* International wires and payouts to non US bank accounts
* Businesses in restricted categories such as gambling, weapons and adult content
